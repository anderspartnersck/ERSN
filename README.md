# ER$N — THE HOME GAME

**Play it in your browser: <https://anderspartnersck.github.io/ERSN/>**

**3D edition:** the same game on a Blender-built board at the gateway table: <https://anderspartnersck.github.io/ERSN/3d.html>

The video edition of the board game. Two to six players, one collapsing market, and a Lounge almost nobody
reaches clean.

**Status:** playable start to finish, one screen only (no online play). The presentation is still rough.

## About

Juice, Trust, Hype and Risk, a Global and a Shadow SEC, and a collapse that comes for everyone. Work the
loop, climb the tower through **REGULATION** and **CORRUPTION**, pass the Trust Check, and get into the
Executive Lounge before the market goes under.

You can't win at fraud. In simulated games of the current Benchmark the market collapses every time, and a
clean win comes up in fewer than one game in two hundred. That is the design, not a bug.

## Editions

- **BENCHMARK** is the default: the digital edition. It has the REGULATION and CORRUPTION tower tiers, the
  rebalanced cards and characters, an SEC meter the players can push and pull, and a three-turn raid when the
  market collapses. It has its own one-page rules sheet.
- **CLASSIC** is the printed game, played as the printed rule sheet and cards are written. Pick it on the setup
  screen.
- The expansion is on by default; there's a switch for it on the setup screen.

## How to play

1. Click or press a key to start, then **ENTER THE TOWER** (or press **Enter**).
2. On setup, pick the number of players (2–6; the official game is 4–6, and 2–3 are a sandbox), the edition,
   the expansion and the board. Each character card has a **YOU / BOT** button: you get seat one, and you can
   turn any other seat into a human for **pass-and-play** at one screen.
3. In the game, **NEXT TURN** (or **Space**) plays the next turn; **AUTO** runs the bots on their own, and the
   slider sets the speed. When a human seat has a decision, the game stops and asks; click your choice.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **Enter** | ENTER THE TOWER on the title |
| **Space** | Next turn |
| **Esc** or **P** | Pause menu: resume, how to play, settings, new game, quit to title |
| **?** or **R** | The rules sheet |
| **Esc** | Closes a sheet or prompt; on setup or the final screen, back to the title |

Left alone on the title for 30 seconds, the game plays a bots-only demo. Any key or click stops it.

**Options** (add to the link): `?b1112=0` plays the Benchmark without the SEC-meter and raid changes ·
`?v4=0` plays the older Benchmark, before the tower tiers and the rebalance · `?attract=0` turns off the title demo.
