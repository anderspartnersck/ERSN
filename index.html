WELCOME TO
THE TOWER

Each player will choose 1 of 6 characters and will manage their personal RISK, JUICE, TRUST, and HYPE — Trust, Hype and Juice have NO CAP (−/+).

Each player will roll off, highest roll leads off, game continuing clockwise.

START TURN

  • Roll 1 six-sided die, gain that much Juice
    Juice is the currency of momentum
  • Resolve any start-of-turn effects

BRIBES: Before moving you may spend 3 Juice to acquire a Bribe
Use a golden token dollar-sign up to track

  • Play to REVERSE a targeted Asset effect or cancel an SEC effect (for you), or buy past a TRUST CHECK into the LOUNGE — BUT every Bribe used adds +2 Risk (and −1 Final Favor)

MOVEMENT

  • Pay 1 Juice to move 1 MARKET LOOP space — backward moves allowed, same fare
  • TO MARKET Door:
  • Pay 1 Juice to enter
  • Saunter to the next space beyond the Tower
  • You may not end your turn on a door space

No 2 players may occupy the same space

  • EXCEPT the START CIRCLE or the EXECUTIVE LOUNGE

MARKET LOOP

  • Draw a card when you move to an ASSET, MARKET, or SEC space on the loop around the Tower
  • Play only 1 card per turn from hand after moving to complete your turn
  • ASSET LINEUP — Played Assets must be separate from hand, face-up or face-down
  • ASSET EFFECTS — You may play 1 effect from an in-play Asset per turn INSTEAD of playing a card

No rewinds for CONSTANTS or card management — The tools of the Tower are in your hand!

TO ENTER THE TOWER,
YOU MUST HAVE 4 OR MORE TRUST OR 8+ HYPE — qualified players walk straight in, no roll.

IF BELOW THRESHOLD, SPEND A BRIBE OR ROLL 1 DIE —
the "WHY ASK?" ROLL:

  • 5–6: SUCCESS
  • 1–4: FAIL, +1 Risk

Benchmark: checks are character-aware — Class Ring passes on 2+, The Safe needs a 6, Raptor Leasing turns a fail into +2 Risk.

BURNOUT

If your Risk reaches 8:

  • Discard or ZERO all Assets, Trust, and Bribes
  • Set Risk to 5, Juice to 1
  • You remain in play — EXPOSED!

COLLAPSE
TRIGGERS

The Global SEC meter will increase by 1 if:

  • A turn ends with total table personal Risk at 8 or above
    (6 or above with fewer than 4 players)
    AND/OR
  • 3 Assets are drawn consecutively (spans turns)

Collapse begins next turn when either of these conditions occur:

  • Global SEC Meter reaches 8
    Use a golden token star-side up to track
  • BENNY BOYE spends 3 consecutive turns in the Tower
  • A collapsed game will end in 3 turns

Expansion: the hidden SHADOW SEC meter is card-driven — collapse also triggers at Shadow SEC 8.

DURING
COLLAPSE

  • On collapse turn 1 each player MUST draw and resolve a SEC Card to end their turn (if any remain)
  • At the end of collapse turns 2–3, players under the Hype or Trust thresholds must use a Bribe or pass an Anders Approved TRUST CHECK to stay in the EXECUTIVE LOUNGE

If failed, move to the START CIRCLE —

  • Security has escorted you from the building

VICTORY CONDITIONS
YOU WIN IF YOU:

  • Are inside the EXECUTIVE LOUNGE
  • Hold 2 in-play, Clean Assets

PRE-COLLAPSE: STAMP THE BOOKS
After 1 full turn, pass a TRUST CHECK
POST-COLLAPSE: SPIRALING
Maintain status for 3 collapse rounds

SHARKY'S DISGUSTING CUBICLE
Strategy, as dictated by Bill "Sharky" Shahkman — thirty-one years on the Anders floor, four non-prosecution agreements, one framed photo of the Tower he will not discuss. He does not have an office. He has a cubicle. It is disgusting. Sit down. Don't touch anything.
🦈 This is the same doctrine the computer seats play by — it's wired into their heads. So when a bot buries you, this is the sheet it read. Read it back.
THE CREED (memorize it, kid)
  1. Embrace the collapse. You will not "win" clean — ninety-nine times in a hundred this building comes down. Stop playing not-to-collapse and start playing to be holding two Clean Assets in the Lounge when it does. The game isn't survival. It's positioning.
  2. Bait the SEC. Global SEC rising isn't your problem if you're already parked upstairs. Let the risk pile up on the table, not on you. A collapse you saw coming is a collapse you win.
  3. Track who holds what. Every face-down Asset is a loaded gun. Remember the reveals, and know exactly who's one Clean Asset from the Lounge. That person is your target — not the loudmouth.
  4. Target the leader, not the noisemaker. The house scores closeness to a clean Lounge win, not Juice or Hype. So should you. Kneecap whoever's about to stamp their books.
  5. Let the politicians spin out while you eat up. Hype is a drug. Watch three players race to MAX Hype and let the bubble math cook them. Keep your Trust at 4 and your head down.
THE MATH THEY DON'T PRINT ON THE BOX
  • The WHY ASK? roll is a coin flip you lose. 5–6 passes = 1 in 3. If you're under threshold and it matters, you don't roll — you Bribe, or you don't go up. Rolling the Tower is for tourists.
  • Every Bribe you spend is +2 Risk on your own head — always — and −1 Final Favor on top. That +2 isn't a fee, it's a fuse: one drags you toward "discovered," two and you're smelling burnout. Spend it desperate or at the wire, when the dice would end you — never for pocket change.
  • Risk 6 is the ledge. Risk 8 is the fall. Ending a turn at Risk ≥ 6 quietly feeds the Shadow meter — that's you pulling the building down, and everyone can see it. Dump Dirty and stop poking the SEC.
  • Sole Juice leader at 8+ pays +1 Risk a turn (Tall Poppy). Conspicuous wealth draws the eye. Sometimes the move is to not be first.
  • Two Clean Assets. Not three, not one-and-a-prayer. Victory checks for exactly the Clean pair in the Lounge. Everything else is set dressing.
THE SIX — know your seat, know theirs
BENNY BOYE
The Golden One
J0 · T7 · H1

The house's favorite son. Golden Walk strolls him into the Lounge FREE at a staircase — no Juice, no roll — and if he ends 3 straight turns up there, Castle Doctrine pulls the pin on the whole game. Benny is the doomsday clock.

VS: He decides when it ends. Be Lounge-ready before he starts his three-count — don't get caught on the stairs when Benny starts humming.

CLAUDIA NUMBERS
The Auditor's Shadow
J2 · T5 · H2

The long game in a pantsuit — starts with the Trust to walk in the front door. Unimpeachable buys Trust by handing a rival Juice; Cooked bites if her Hype hits 4+. She never celebrates. That's how you know she already won.

VS: Low-variance. You beat her by getting there first, not by out-grinding her.

VONDA VOUCH
The Endorser
J4 · T3 · H5

Launders garbage into gold. The Stamp counts two face-up Dirty as one Clean (if nothing's face-down), and Leaky Bucket lets her cling to the Lounge through a collapse eviction. Underdog on paper. She is not.

VS: She needs the Dirty pile intact — disrupt it before she stamps.

DANNY DOUGH
The Runaway Cycle
J2 · T2 · H2

The compounding machine. Dough-Man Cometh doubles his Juice every other turn; he hoards, protects the spiral, never spends down early. His sheet is an illusion — put him in the red and force the early Bribes.

VS: Hit him before turn 4. Late, he's a runaway train with a caboose full of money.

WU DRAINER
The Absent-Minded
J5 · T3 · H2

The wrecking ball. Floorplan Master ignores movement blocks and moves free; Strategic Fade shaves Risk when Wu sits still. Wu doesn't build. Wu demolishes — and boxes out whoever's ahead.

VS: Don't leave loaded face-down Assets where a disruptor can knock them over.

MARK MARKIT
The Belief Engine
J3 · T3 · H5

Pure narrative. Hype-Man pumps Hype off every Market card; he rides the Hype ≥ 8 bridge straight into the Lounge and skips the Trust game entirely. He's on fire and he thinks it's the spotlight.

VS: Fragile at the top — one bad SEC card or a Trust crater and the balloon pops.
